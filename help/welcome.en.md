# Welcome to Voice Commands

You speak an instruction, and Claude Code carries it out on your computer.

- **{toggle}** starts recording. Press it again to send the command.
- **{cancel}** cancels, also shortly after recognition in case something was misheard.
- **{send}** sends the recognized command right away, without waiting.
- **{type}** opens a text field if you would rather type a command.
- **{window}** opens this window with Claude's responses.

Before Claude reads or changes anything outside its own folder `~/Voice-Commands`, or runs a command, it asks you with a yes or no window.

Try it: press **{toggle}**, say for example "What time is it in Tokyo?" and press **{toggle}** again.

The **help** behind the question mark at the top explains everything else, and the settings are behind the gear.
